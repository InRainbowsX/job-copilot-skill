# Product Management Template

Apply this template when the candidate is really being judged as a `产品经理 / 产品运营偏产品 / 解决方案设计` style product candidate rather than a technical or broad operations profile.

Use this template together with:

- [job-families.md](./job-families.md)
- [review-rubric.md](./review-rubric.md)

## Role Definition

Strong product candidates usually show:

- clear user problem definition
- business goal or metric orientation
- requirement breakdown and solution design
- cross-team alignment and delivery push
- result review and iteration logic

## Routing Signals

Prefer this template when the materials emphasize:

- PRD, requirement design, feature definition, roadmap, business analysis
- user interviews, problem discovery, product方案, priority tradeoffs
- coordination with design, engineering, operations, sales, or business teams
- launch results, usage, conversion, retention, or process efficiency outcomes

## Resume Packaging Coach Prompts

Ask enough to pin down:

- what user or business problem existed
- who proposed the direction and who approved it
- what the candidate personally owned in discovery, design, push, and review
- what tradeoffs were made and why
- what result or learning came after launch

High-value deep-dive prompts:

- What exact user problem or business bottleneck did this project target?
- How did you decide what to do first and what not to do?
- What part did you personally define versus only coordinate?
- Which teams did you need to align and what resistance existed?
- What happened after launch, and what did you change next?

## Packaging Rules

Prefer this structure:

`problem / goal -> product decision -> cross-team push -> result / review`

Good product packaging should emphasize:

- user or business problem clarity
- decision quality, not just task listing
- delivery push across functions
- launch result plus review logic

Safe upgrade moves:

- turn vague “负责需求” into explicit problem-definition and decision ownership when support exists
- connect scattered meetings and coordination into a delivery narrative
- convert feature listing into business-goal-oriented packaging

High-risk moves:

- presenting pure coordination as full product ownership
- claiming roadmap or strategy ownership without clear scope
- writing launch results with no metric or observation window

## Review Focus

The review officer should pressure-test:

- whether the candidate can explain the original problem and why it mattered
- whether the candidate can explain the tradeoffs made
- whether ownership over requirement design and launch is real
- whether cross-team push was substantive or just attendance
- whether results and reviews are specific enough to defend

## Mock Interview Themes

Use product-specific follow-up such as:

1. What user problem did you actually solve?
2. Why was this the right priority?
3. What alternatives did you reject, and why?
4. What did you personally own in requirement design and coordination?
5. What result after launch convinced you the decision was right or wrong?
