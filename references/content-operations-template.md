# Content Operations / Self-Media Template

Apply this fine-grained template when the broader operations route is correct, but the resume is really being judged as `内容运营 / 自媒体运营 / 个人内容项目`.

Use this template together with:

- [operations-template.md](./operations-template.md)
- [content-operations-knowledge-base.md](./content-operations-knowledge-base.md)

## Strong Signals

- clear audience positioning and content niche
- topic-selection logic, content pillars, or recurring editorial themes
- platform-aware production and distribution decisions
- evidence of review loops, not one-off posting
- downstream linkage to engagement, retention, lead flow, private traffic, or conversion
- personal creation periods that still show sustained output, feedback, and transferable content capability

## Expression Structure

Prefer this shape:

`audience / goal -> content system or action -> distribution / iteration -> result or downstream value`

Good bullets should usually answer:

- who the content served
- how the topic or content direction was chosen
- what production or distribution action happened
- what the data or user feedback changed

## Result Strategy

Prefer results such as:

- reads, views, watch time, open rate, interaction, share, save
- follower growth or audience growth with observation window
- community activity or repeat consumption
- leads, private-traffic accumulation, registrations, conversion or participation

If downstream conversion is weak, do not fake it. Keep the story around:

- audience fit
- engagement quality
- stable output and iteration ability

## Upgrade Path

Typical upgrade paths:

- from `负责账号运营` to `围绕目标受众搭建内容定位和选题节奏`
- from `产出内容` to `说明选题、生产、分发和复盘闭环`
- from `涨粉 / 阅读提升` to `说明哪些内容动作驱动了哪些结果`
- from `个人创作` to `gap 中持续的个人内容项目` when there is real continuity and output

## High-Risk Phrasing

Downgrade or challenge these when unsupported:

- full content strategy ownership with only execution support
- account-level growth written as purely personal output
- vanity metrics with no observation window or business meaning
- platform luck written as a stable methodology
- personal creation period written as formal employment

## Review Focus

The review officer should pressure-test:

- whether the candidate can explain the audience and positioning
- whether the candidate can explain why the topic direction worked
- whether the content process was continuous and structured
- whether account-level outcomes and direct contribution are separated
- whether personal creation periods are packaged truthfully

## Mock Interview Follow-Up Chain

High-value follow-up sequence:

1. Who exactly was the target audience?
2. How did you choose topics or content pillars?
3. What changed after seeing the first round of feedback?
4. Which platform or channel mattered most, and why?
5. Which result can truly be attributed to your own action?
6. If this was a personal creation period, how did you keep output stable and what did it teach you?
