# Sales Template

Apply this template when the candidate targets internet sales roles such as B2B sales, solution sales, channel sales, inside sales, account management, or renewal and upsell positions.

## Role Definition

This family is judged on whether the candidate can explain customer type, pipeline movement, deal conversion, and what personal actions led to revenue, renewal, or account expansion.

Strong sales candidates usually show:

- a clear customer segment
- specific responsibility in the sales process
- deal or pipeline movement caused by their actions
- repeatable conversion methods
- quota, revenue, renewal, or expansion results they can defend

## Routing Signals

Prefer this family when the materials emphasize:

- customer acquisition, lead follow-up, demos, proposals, contract negotiation
- key accounts, channel expansion, renewal, upsell, pipeline management
- revenue, quota, signing, conversion, close rate, sales cycle

Common keywords:

- pipeline, MQL, SQL, lead, deal, quota, close rate, GMV, ARR, renewal
- proposal, contract, negotiation, demo, onboarding, key account, upsell
- channel, distribution, customer expansion, win rate, revenue contribution

## Agent 1 Deep-Dive Prompts

Always separate target customer, stage ownership, and attributable result.

### Shared Sales Questions

- What customer type did you focus on?
- What part of the sales cycle did you own?
- How were leads sourced or assigned?
- What were the main blockers to conversion?
- How did you move deals forward?
- What metrics were used to evaluate performance?
- What was your quota, target, or expected outcome?
- Which results were personal, team-level, or account-level?

### Account Growth / Renewal Add-On Questions

- How did you maintain the relationship after the initial deal?
- What renewal or expansion signal did you look for?
- How did you identify upsell opportunities?
- What objections did customers raise, and how did you respond?

## Packaging Rules

Emphasize:

- customer segment and deal context
- your action to move the pipeline
- measurable business result

Good sales wording usually follows:

`customer -> action -> conversion movement -> revenue or renewal result`

Preferred packaging angles:

- conversion improvement
- key account development
- objection handling and negotiation
- renewal and upsell
- repeatable pipeline management

Safe upgrade moves:

- sharpen vague communication work into clear sales-stage actions
- connect meetings, demos, and follow-ups into an explicit conversion chain
- describe method or playbook when the candidate can explain it

High-risk moves:

- claiming team quota or region performance as personal revenue
- inflating supporting actions into full deal ownership
- overstating strategic account planning without customer evidence
- writing big revenue numbers with no territory, cycle, or attribution context

## Agent 2 Review Focus

In addition to the shared rubric, check:

- whether the candidate can define the customer type and buying path
- whether revenue or conversion numbers are clearly attributable
- whether personal ownership in the pipeline is distinct from team support
- whether renewal or upsell claims are backed by specific actions
- whether the candidate can explain objections, blockers, and close logic

Downgrade when:

- the candidate cannot explain how a deal advanced
- quota or revenue language is unsupported
- account ownership is larger in writing than in the candidate's explanation

## Mock Interview Themes

Use sales-specific pressure tests such as:

- What kind of customers did you convert best, and why?
- Walk me through a difficult deal from first contact to close.
- Which part did you personally own in that result?
- How did you respond when a customer objected on price or timing?
- How do you judge whether a renewal or upsell opportunity is real?
- Which metric best reflects your effectiveness, and what did you do to improve it?
