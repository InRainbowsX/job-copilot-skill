# Review Rubric

Agent 2 uses this shared rubric before anything becomes final wording.

## Decision Labels

- `pass`: strong enough to enter the final version
- `pass after supplement`: direction is good, but support is incomplete
- `rewrite required`: too risky, too vague, or mismatched to the role

## Required Checks

### 1. Fact Consistency

Check whether the new wording conflicts with:

- original resume
- self-introduction
- candidate clarifications
- stored memory

### 2. Responsibility Boundary

Check whether the wording accurately reflects:

- what the candidate led
- what the candidate implemented
- what the team owned
- what the candidate only supported

### 3. Explainability

Reject wording the candidate probably cannot explain under follow-up:

- why this mattered
- why this approach was chosen
- what was difficult
- how the result was measured

### 4. Role Fit

Check whether the project now sounds like a real candidate for the routed job family.

### 5. Result Credibility

Challenge:

- dramatic metrics with no baseline
- vague impact with no observable value
- team-level outcomes presented as individual outcomes

### 6. Interview Survivability

Ask whether the story can survive 3 to 5 follow-up questions. If not, downgrade or request more support.

## Risk Levels

- `A`: safe to use now
- `B`: usable after a small clarification
- `C`: high-risk; requires downgrade or major supplement
- `D`: do not use

## Mandatory Output Per Review

For each project card, output:

- decision label
- risk level
- top 3 risks
- missing info to collect
- downgrade wording if needed
- likely interview pressure points
